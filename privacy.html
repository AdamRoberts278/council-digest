Privacy policy

Council digest is a personal tool used by one person, its author. It is not offered to the public.

What it accesses

With the author's consent, it has read-only access to the author's own Gmail account (the gmail.readonly permission). It reads the sender, subject and a short preview of recent inbox messages.

What it does with that data

The messages are processed by an AI model running on the author's own computer, at home. The result is a short summary kept on that computer. Nothing is sent to any other person, company or online service.

What it does not do

It does not send, change or delete email. It does not sell, share or publish any data, and it does not use any data for advertising or for training AI models.

Retention and control

Summaries stay on the author's computer until the author deletes them. The author can remove the tool's access at any time from the Google Account permissions page.
